Common questions

Good to know

What are your hours?

We are open every day of the week, 10 am to 5 pm — including most holidays. Holiday hours can differ, so call ahead at 419-917-1706 if you are making a special trip.

Where exactly are you?

1760 Woodville Rd, Millbury, Ohio 43447 — a roadside farm stand on Woodville Road in Wood County, a short drive from Toledo, Oregon, Northwood, Walbridge, Perrysburg and Genoa.

Do you ship, or is it pickup only?

Local pickup only. We take orders from anywhere and will set them aside for you, but everything is collected at the farm — we do not ship.

What payment methods do you take?

Cash, all major credit cards, PayPal, Venmo, Zelle and Cash App.

Do you post prices?

No. Prices follow the market and change through the season, so call for today's pricing rather than relying on an out-of-date list.

Do I need to call before coming?

Not for a general visit — we are open daily. Call first if you want something specific held, or if you are after a seasonal item like duck, turkey or goose eggs.
