Common questions

Good to know

What live birds do you sell?

Chickens, turkeys, ducks and geese — in more breeds and colors than you will typically find at a feed store. Availability changes with what is coming up in the barn, so call 419-917-1706 to hear what is ready now.

Can I buy just a few hens for my backyard?

Yes. Whether you want three laying hens for the backyard or a breeding trio of geese, you are welcome. Come pick your birds in person.

How do I know which breed suits my setup?

Ask us. We keep most of these breeds ourselves, so we can tell you honestly how they handle winter, how broody they go, and what to expect for laying.

How are the birds raised?

On straw and fresh air, here at the farm. They are raised by Joe and Margie Kill — it is a family farm in the truest sense, and it shows in the animals.

Do I need an appointment?

No appointment needed during our hours, 10 am to 5 pm daily. A call ahead is smart though, so you know what is available before you make the drive.
