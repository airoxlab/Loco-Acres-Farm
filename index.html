From our hives

Raw, unfiltered, truly local honey.

Our honey goes from the hive to the jar and nowhere in between — never heated, never filtered, so it keeps all the pollen, enzymes, and flavor of the fields the bees actually worked.

Local honey means local blooms. Every jar tastes like this corner of Wood County, and no two seasons are quite the same.

Jarred at the farm from our own hives — while the harvest lasts.

Fresh daily

Four kinds of eggs, one farm stand.

Most stands stop at chicken eggs. Ours is where bakers come for rich duck eggs, and the curious come to try a turkey egg or crack a single goose egg into the pan for breakfast.

All four are available now — gathered from birds that spend their days scratching, foraging, and dabbling around the farm.

Pasture to freezer

Freezer meats in stock: beef, turkey, chicken, and rabbit.

Our freezer meats — beef, turkey, chicken, and rabbit — are raised right here, not trucked in. Duck, goose, and guinea fowl come in smaller numbers, so we sell those by preorder to make sure every bird finds its table.

Tell us what you're planning and we'll set it aside. A quick call is all it takes to reserve.

Start your own flock

Live poultry, straight from our barn.

We sell live chickens, turkeys, ducks, geese, and other poultry — healthy birds raised on straw and fresh air, in more breeds and colors than you'll find at a feed store. Whether you want three laying hens for the backyard or a breeding trio of geese, come pick your birds in person.

Every bird here is raised by Joe and Margie Kill — a family farm in the truest sense, and it shows in the animals.

Not sure what breed fits your setup? Ask — we keep most of them ourselves.

Thanksgiving & Christmas

Holiday birds sell out weeks in advance. Order early.

Every year, our holiday turkeys, geese, ducks, and guinea fowl are spoken for long before the holidays arrive. We start accepting Thanksgiving and Christmas orders as early as July — and we always sell out.
