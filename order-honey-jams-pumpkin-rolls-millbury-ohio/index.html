From our kitchen and our hives

Made here by Joe & Margie.

Our raw honey comes from our own hives on Woodville Road. It's never heated and never filtered, so it tastes like this corner of Wood County.

Margie makes our jams and jellies all year. Peach habanero is the one people drive out for, with strawberry habanero right behind it. Her pumpkin rolls show up again and again in our Google reviews, and they're made to order, so you get one fresh.

Pay ahead online and your order is set aside for you. Just call ahead before you come by, so we're sure to be home.

Common questions

Good to know

How much is your raw honey?

1 lb is $10 and 2 lb is $20 in our classic squeeze bottle, or $2 extra in glass. A quart (3 lb) is $28 and a half gallon (6 lb) is $58, plus a $2 deposit on the glass jar.

Do I pay when I order?

Yes. Honey, jams and pumpkin rolls are paid in full when you order, by card or Cash App through Square, or with PayPal, Venmo or Zelle. Your total is added up for you as you order.

How do I order Margie's pumpkin rolls?

Add them to your order on this page. Pumpkin rolls are $15 for a 2 lb roll. They are made to order, so we let you know when yours is ready to pick up.

Which jams do you make?

Our most popular are peach habanero, strawberry habanero, strawberry, peach and strawberry jalapeño, all $7 for an 8 oz jar, with more flavors coming. Jams and jellies are made year round.

Do you ship or deliver?

No. Everything is local pickup only, at the farm, 1760 Woodville Rd in Millbury, Ohio. Please call ahead.
