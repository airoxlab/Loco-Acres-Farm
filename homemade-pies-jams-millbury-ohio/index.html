Pies & cookies

Most of them, all year long.

Most of our pies and cookies are available year round. Some pies are seasonal and only show up when the fruit is ready: elderberry, raspberry and gooseberry among them.

Stock changes day to day, so if you have your heart set on a particular pie, call 419-917-1706 before you drive out and we will tell you what's on hand or set one aside.

Common questions

Good to know

Are your pies available year round?

Most of our pies and cookies are available year round. Some pies are seasonal, like elderberry, raspberry and gooseberry, and are made when the fruit is ready.

What jams and jellies do you make?

Jams and jellies are made year round. Our five most popular are peach habanero, strawberry habanero, strawberry, peach, and strawberry jalapeño.

Can I order a pie ahead of time?

Call 419-917-1706 to check what is on hand today or to ask about a particular pie. We will set it aside for you.

Do you ship pies or jams?

No. Everything we sell is local pickup only at the farm stand at 1760 Woodville Rd in Millbury, Ohio.
