Common questions

Good to know

When is the deadline to order a Thanksgiving turkey?

Thanksgiving orders close November 20. Pickup at the farm starts Monday, November 23. Christmas orders close December 18, with pickup on December 21.

How much are your holiday turkeys, geese and ducks?

Turkeys are $5 per pound, geese are $9 per pound and ducks are $7 per pound. The final price depends on the weight of your bird at pickup.

What sizes can I get?

Turkeys range from about 14 to 36 lb, geese from 8 to 20 lb and ducks from 4 to 12 lb. Tell us the size you would like; we cannot guarantee an exact size, and orders are filled in the order they are received.

Are the holiday birds fresh or frozen?

Holiday birds are fresh for pickup at the farm. Our year-round freezer turkey and chicken are a separate thing and are stocked in the freezer.

Do you ship or deliver?

No. Holiday birds are local pickup only, at the farm stand at 1760 Woodville Rd in Millbury, Ohio.

Is it too late to order?

Call 419-917-1706 and ask. Orders are filled in the order they are received, and we would rather tell you straight than have you assume.
